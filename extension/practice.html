Checking Dictozy settings…

Your words appear here. You can edit them or use your usual Undo shortcut. Nothing is submitted.

Prefer the keyboard?

Your shortcut: Loading. On supported webpages it starts or stops recording, and cancels a pending transcription.

You can also Tab to the microphone and press Enter or Space. Tab to Stop or Discard during recording.

You control the microphone

Opening this page or selecting Try dictation does not record. Recording starts only after you activate the microphone or assigned shortcut. The default limit is 10 seconds; you can choose up to 30 seconds in the popup.

Audio goes to Dictozy’s backend and xAI for transcription. Existing field text is not sent. There is no saved recording or transcript history; the backend may use temporary upload files. A result that cannot be inserted safely can be copied for 60 seconds.

Use a non-sensitive phrase for practice. Returning to another tab stops recording and clears temporary recovery.

Microphone or dictation not working?

Check that Dictozy is on in the popup. Allow microphone access when prompted, and check Chrome and your operating system’s microphone settings if access is blocked. Make sure the correct microphone is connected.

Websites request their own microphone permission and some block recording. If practice works but a website does not, try a normal text field on another HTTPS page. Switching tabs or moving to an unsafe field cancels recording.

If the field changes while a result is pending, Dictozy preserves your edits and offers Copy or Discard. If Copy fails, keep this page active and try again before the result expires.
