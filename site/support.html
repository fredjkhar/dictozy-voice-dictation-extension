Support and troubleshooting

Start with the checks below when a control, recording, or transcription does not behave as expected. For a guided introduction, explore the interactive guide without using your microphone.

Quick checks

  1. Open the Dictozy popup and confirm the main Dictozy toggle is on.
  2. Confirm Enable on this site is on for the current website.
  3. Reload the page after installing or updating Dictozy, then focus a supported field again.
  4. Check that the field is not sensitive, hidden, disabled, or readonly.
  5. In the popup, select Try dictation to open the practice page. It requests microphone access only when you choose to start recording.

Microphone control or signal

The microphone button does not appear

Confirm the global and current-site toggles are enabled. The button appears only beside a supported, editable field. Chrome's own pages, the Chrome Web Store, browser settings, password fields, payment fields, and other excluded fields intentionally do not receive the control.

Microphone access is blocked

Select the site information control in your browser's address bar, review the site's microphone permission, and allow access only if you intend to dictate there. Reload the page after changing permission. Dictozy never starts recording automatically.

No microphone is found or the microphone is busy

Confirm your browser is using the intended input, the microphone is connected and not muted, and another application is not preventing access. Microphone quality, background noise, pronunciation, and browser or device behavior can affect recognition. Try a short phrase in a quiet space.

Keyboard shortcut

If the popup shows Not assigned, use its keyboard control to open Chrome's extension-shortcut settings, then assign or remap the Dictozy command. The shortcut starts, stops, or cancels the same dictation lifecycle as the visible control.

A shortcut is ignored when Dictozy is disabled globally, disabled for the current site, or the focused field is unsupported.

Recording or transcription failure

Cancel and retry

Use the cancel control while transcription is pending. After a failure, use the retry state to record a fresh clip; Dictozy does not resend the previous audio. Keep the phrase short and wait for the status to finish before moving focus.

Check service availability

Dictozy distinguishes a clear offline state, a connection failure, a busy service, a timeout, and a temporary transcription failure. Follow the displayed action, then make a fresh recording. If repeated retries fail, wait briefly and try again.

A short reference appears only after a transcription request starts. Keep that reference and the approximate time, but do not include the spoken text or page contents.

Text was not inserted

If the original field changes, Dictozy can offer Copy and Discard instead of overwriting the draft. Keep the tab visible and choose Copy within 60 seconds, then paste where you want the result. If the clipboard is unavailable, retry before expiry. Cancelled recordings have no recoverable result.

Personal vocabulary and sharing

Save words or short phrases under Personal vocabulary in the popup. Saving keeps them in Chrome. Only enabling vocabulary assistance sends them with future dictations to Dictozy’s backend and xAI. A hint does not guarantee a spelling. Disable sharing to keep your list local, or clear it to delete the list.

Supported and excluded fields

Dictozy supports text, search, email, URL, and telephone inputs; textareas; supported contenteditable fields; and ARIA textboxes that are actually editable. Some complex rich-text editors may manage selection differently from standard fields.

Dictozy intentionally ignores password, payment, hidden, disabled, readonly, file, checkbox, and radio fields, plus bare non-editable ARIA textboxes. It also does not run on restricted Chrome pages.

Report a problem safely

Email support at dictozy.support@gmail.com. You can also open the published Dictozy Chrome Web Store listing and use the support option shown there. Chrome may require you to sign in before submitting feedback.

Helpful, non-sensitive details include:

  • Chrome version, operating system, and Dictozy version from chrome://extensions.
  • Whether Dictozy came from the Chrome Web Store or was loaded unpacked.
  • The general kind of field involved and the steps that led to the issue.
  • A safe screenshot of Dictozy's status, an approximate time, and any short error reference.

Never submit raw audio, transcript text, passwords, payment data, API keys, private page content, existing field contents, or browser storage dumps.

Common questions

Dictozy FAQ

Where does Dictozy work?

On supported editable fields on Chrome HTTPS pages. Restricted browser pages and excluded field types are intentionally unsupported.

Does recording start automatically?

No. Recording begins only after you click the visible microphone button or press your assigned browser shortcut.

When is microphone permission requested?

Chrome requests microphone access only after you start dictation from the visible control or assigned shortcut. Dictozy does not request access automatically in the background.

Is audio or transcript history stored?

The extension and backend application do not intentionally persist raw audio or transcripts, and Dictozy does not provide transcript history. Service providers may retain operational data under their policies.

Does Dictozy work offline?

No. It needs an internet connection and an available backend because speech-to-text processing occurs through the backend and its provider.

Why might the microphone button not appear?

Dictozy may be disabled globally or for that site, the page may need reloading, or the focused field or page may be intentionally unsupported.

What if the keyboard shortcut conflicts with another command?

Use the keyboard control in the Dictozy popup to open Chrome's extension shortcut settings, then assign another available shortcut.

What does language formatting change?

An explicit language guides provider formatting for items such as numbers, currencies, and units. It is a formatting hint, not a promise of better recognition.

Can Dictozy be disabled on one site?

Yes. Turn off Enable on this site in the popup. Dictozy stores only exact origins you explicitly disable in local Chrome extension storage.

Is an account required?

No. Dictozy does not require a user account.
