How it works

From a thought to a draft.

One short clip at a time, in the field you chose.

  1. 01

    Choose a field

    Click where you want to write on a supported HTTPS page. Look for the microphone beside the field.

  2. 02

    Speak, then stop

    Click the microphone or use your assigned shortcut to start. Select Stop to transcribe or Discard to drop the recording.

  3. 03

    Keep editing

    Your words appear at the intended caret or selection when insertion is safe. Use your usual Undo in supported editors.

Recording

See the time you have.

See elapsed time, time remaining, and mic activity. The limit is 10 seconds by default and adjustable up to 30. Stop early whenever you like.

Recovery

Keep the draft you edited.

If the field changes while you wait, Dictozy can offer Copy or Discard for one completed transcript for up to 60 seconds. Cancelled dictation cannot be recovered.

Vocabulary

Choose which words to share.

Save names or terms locally. A separate switch lets you send them with future requests as hints to xAI. Saving alone leaves sharing off; a hint cannot guarantee a spelling.

Setup

Make it fit your writing.

Open Try dictation in the popup to practice. Adjust language formatting, remap your shortcut, or turn Dictozy off for a particular site.

Privacy and control

Where your words go.

Read the privacy policy →

01 In your browser

Recording starts only after you activate it. Your existing field text and surrounding page contents stay out of the transcription request.

02 For transcription

Your clip travels over HTTPS to Dictozy’s backend, which calls xAI Speech-to-Text. Saved vocabulary accompanies it only if you enabled sharing.

03 Back in your field

Dictozy returns the result to the selected field when safe. It offers no permanent transcript history.

Compatibility

Before you dictate.

Start with ordinary text fields in Chrome. Complex editors can handle focus and Undo differently.

Visit support →
Which fields can I use?

Use text, search, email, URL, or telephone inputs, textareas, and supported rich-text editors. Dictozy skips password and payment fields. Selected Gmail and ChatGPT workflows have been tested; Outlook, Notion, and WhatsApp remain unverified.

When does Dictozy request microphone access?

Only when you start dictation with the microphone control or assigned shortcut. Opening the popup or practice page does not start a recording.

What if the keyboard shortcut is already in use?

Open the shortcut control in the popup and choose an available key combination in Chrome. You can also Tab to the microphone and press Enter or Space.

How are audio and transcripts handled?

Dictozy’s backend sends user-initiated audio to xAI for transcription. Larger uploads can use temporary files. See the storage and retention details for how the application and providers handle data.

Do I need an account?

No. Install Dictozy from the Chrome Web Store to dictate, or try the simulated guide without installing anything.
